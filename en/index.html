Good to know.

Is every balcony suitable?+

No. Space, safety, hygiene, neighbours and proper animal care all matter. BalconyFarm is an invitation to explore an idea, not a claim that every balcony is suitable.

Where should I start?+

First check the conditions and rules in your location. Then plan care, costs and shelter. Only then decide whether to keep animals.

Does the calculator show sales profit?+

It shows the value of eggs minus the costs you enter. If you use the eggs at home, this compares their value to you, not cash income. Check the applicable rules separately before selling.

Is egg production constant?+

No. Production varies with age, season, health and living conditions, among other factors. Adjust the calculator assumptions and compare several scenarios, including less favourable ones.
